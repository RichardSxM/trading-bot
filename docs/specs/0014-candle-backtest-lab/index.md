# 0014. Backtest hour level strategies on Kraken's 1 minute candle history, through the same engine and strategy code as the paper loop

**Date**: 2026-10-17
**Status**: In Progress

## Summary

The candle lab downloads Kraken Futures' free 1 minute BTC perp candles since the contract launched (March 2022) into a Parquet folder on the laptop. Funding rates come from two free Kraken sources merged into the same folder: a one time import of Kraken's funding export (every rate since launch, 4 hourly until late September 2022, hourly after) and the rolling one year funding endpoint on every fetch. It then backtests hour level strategies over any date range from 2022-03-24. Each minute with trades becomes four price points (open, the two extremes, close) fed to the NautilusTrader engine as quotes and trades. A minute with no trades becomes a single quote, so no volume is ever made up. The exact strategy classes the paper trader runs build their own hour bars and trade unchanged. Fees, a fixed slippage per fill, hourly funding, and forced liquidation are charged. A look ahead check and a candle versus order book parity check prove the results can be trusted. Results land in the same Postgres tables and reports as the scalping simulator, ready for the promotion gate (feature 15).

## Requirements

**User stories**:
- As the trader, I want to backtest an hour level strategy over years of real Kraken BTC perp history, with fees, funding, and slippage, so I only paper trade strategies whose edge survives costs.
- As the trader, I want the backtest to run the very strategy class the paper loop runs, so a backtest result says something about paper and live.
- As the trader, I want a strategy that peeks at the future to fail loudly, so a too good result is never a look ahead bug.
- As the trader, I want buy and hold and a simple MACD baseline scored the same way, so every candidate has a benchmark to beat.
- As the trader, I want to sweep a few parameter values in one command, so I can compare settings without editing files by hand.

**Acceptance criteria**:
- **AC-1** (candle fetch): `lab fetch-candles <from> <to>` takes UTC months (`YYYY-MM`, both inclusive). A month before `2022-03` or after the current UTC month is refused before any request (exit 64). For each month in order it pages the Kraken Futures charts endpoint for `trade` candles of `PF_XBTUSD` at resolution `1m`, from the month's start to the month's end or the fetch's start time, whichever is earlier, and writes `catalog-lab/candles/data/bar/PF_XBTUSD.KRAKEN-1-MINUTE-LAST-EXTERNAL/<YYYY-MM>.parquet`. Each row is a Nautilus `Bar` with open, high, low, close as `Price` at the pinned instrument's price precision and volume as `Quantity` at its size precision, and `ts_event` = `ts_init` = the minute's close (its open + 60 s), sorted by `ts_init`, one row per minute Kraken returns, plus a boolean `quiet` column. Volume strings carry float noise (for example `0.3565999999999999`): a volume within `1e-8` of a multiple of the size step is rounded to that multiple (`ROUND_HALF_EVEN`) and counted in the manifest's `volume_rounded_rows`, and one further off is a bad row (AC-3). A minute is quiet when its volume after rounding is 0 and its open, high, low, and close are all equal (a raw `0` from Kraken, or noise that rounds to 0 on a flat candle). It is stored with `quiet = true`, checked flat at the previous close by AC-3, and is not a missing minute. A candle whose volume rounds to 0 but is not flat is kept as a trade minute (`quiet = false`, volume 0); the stream gives it step size trades (AC-9) and the manifest counts it in `zero_volume_trade_rows`. Prices are whole numbers on tick 1, with two exceptions the full history fetch found in 2023-08 to 2024-01 (rationale.md, *Off tick prices*). A price within `1e-8` of a tick (inclusive, the same bound as volume) is float noise (for example `39522.99999999999`) and is snapped to that tick (`ROUND_HALF_EVEN`), in any of the four fields. A high or low further off the tick is rounded toward the candle's body: a high down to the tick (`ROUND_FLOOR`), a low up to it (`ROUND_CEILING`). So apart from a noise snap (at most `1e-8`), no stored price lies beyond the range Kraken reported, and the stored candle reaches every whole tick price exactly when Kraken's did, which keeps stops and limits on the tick firing as they would have. An open or close further off than the noise is a bad row (AC-3), never rounded. The order is fixed: snap and round all four prices first, then flag an off tick open or close, then run every other AC-3 check, the quiet decision, and the duplicate rule on the stored values. A candle that is not a bad row and had any price snapped or rounded counts once in the manifest's `price_rounded_rows`, however many fields changed. After the checks pass, the command logs INFO `lab_candles_price_rounded` for a month with any rounded candle: the month, `price_rounded_rows`, and up to 50 of the real roundings (minute, field, raw value, stored value); noise snaps are only counted. Rows that repeat across page edges are kept once when their stored values are identical (copies that differ only in noise are one row). A month whose end is before the fetch's start is written with `complete: true`. The running month gets `complete: false` and is fetched fresh on every run, and so is any month whose manifest says `complete: false`. A complete month is skipped (`lab_candles_month_skipped`) unless `--refetch` is given. `manifests/<YYYY-MM>.json` is written last, as the month's commit point. A month killed partway (no manifest) is fetched again from scratch. The pinned instrument has `price_increment` 1 (precision 0) and `size_increment` 0.0001. The first minute Kraken has is 2022-03-23 10:03 UTC (gate G-1), so month `2022-03` is fetched but no backtest range may start before 2022-03-24 (AC-6).
- **AC-2** (funding fetch): the same command fetches Kraken's historical funding rates for `PF_XBTUSD` (one public request, kept for the whole command; the endpoint is a rolling window of about one year and ignores date parameters) and merges its rows into `data/funding_rate_update/PF_XBTUSD.KRAKEN/<YYYY-MM>.parquet`, where `<YYYY-MM>` is the UTC month of the row's charge time (`ts_init`, so a stamp of 2022-04-30 20:00 lands in 2022-05), for every month the rows fall in, whether or not that month's candles are fetched in this run. Each row is a Nautilus `FundingRateUpdate` with `rate` = Kraken's relative funding rate (parsed with `parse_float=Decimal`), `ts_event` = `ts_init` = the charge time, and `next_funding_ns` = the charge time + the period, plus a `source` column (`export` or `api`, the source that first stored the row; a later agreeing row from the other source leaves it unchanged). Kraken stamps a rate with the start of the period it applies to (gate G-2), so the charge time = stamp + period. The period is 4 h for a stamp before `FUNDING_HOURLY_FROM` (2022-09-29 12:00 UTC, a named constant in `trading_core/lab/candles.py`) and 1 h from it on. The 4 h era's rate meaning (charged once per 4 h period, or a per hour rate) is settled by gate G-4 before Milestone 2. The merge rule, shared with AC-2a: rows are keyed by charge time; a stored row is never deleted, also when the endpoint no longer returns it; a new row is added; a row both stored and fetched must have the same rate, otherwise it is a bad row (AC-3). A month's funding file is written before its manifest, and the manifest's funding fields (`funding_rows`, `funding_sources`, `missing_funding_charges`) are recomputed from the stored rows and rewritten with it. A complete month keeps `complete: true`, `fetched_at`, and its candles untouched. The expected charge times come from one pure function, `expected_charge_times(start_ns, end_ns)` in `trading_core/lab/candles.py` (every whole hour from `FUNDING_HOURLY_FROM` on, every 4 h UTC boundary before it), used by AC-4, AC-6, and AC-15 alike.
- **AC-2a** (funding import): `lab import-funding <file>` reads Kraken's funding export (the ZIP from Kraken support's "Export historical funding rates" page, which you download yourself), taking only the member `exports/PF_XBTUSD.csv`, or a bare CSV with the same columns. The header must be `timestamp,tradeable,absolute_rate,relative_rate` and every `tradeable` must be `PF_XBTUSD`. `timestamp` is a naive UTC `YYYY-MM-DD HH:MM:SS` (the stamp), `relative_rate` is parsed as `Decimal`, and `absolute_rate` is ignored. Every row gets the AC-3 funding checks and is merged with the AC-2 rule and stamp to charge time conversion. A missing file, a ZIP without the member, a wrong header, or another tradeable exits 64 before anything is written. Bad rows or a disagreement with a stored row exit 1 with ERROR `lab_candles_bad_rows`, and nothing is written for any month (all rows are checked before the first write). If the process dies partway through the writes, some months are merged and some not; running the import again finishes it, because the merge adds only what is missing, and until then AC-6 refuses any range that still lacks rows. If gate G-4 finds that the 4 h era rate is a per hour rate, the import stores `relative_rate × 4` for every stamp before `FUNDING_HOURLY_FROM`, so every stored rate means "charged once at its charge time" everywhere (funding payments, `market_context()`, and `check_entry`'s funding cost), and rationale.md records the finding. It writes one funding file per touched month (atomic, the AC-2 manifest rule; a month with no manifest yet gets its funding file only, and its manifest is written by `fetch-candles`), never touches candles, makes no network request, runs with `--no-deps`, and logs `lab_funding_imported` with rows added, rows already present, and months touched. Running it twice adds nothing the second time. The export checked on 2026-10-17 holds 30,431 rows from 2022-03-22 16:00 to 2026-02-01 00:00, and its 2,604 rows that overlap the endpoint match it exactly.
- **AC-3** (row checks): before a month is written, every candle is checked: open, high, low, close all above zero and on the instrument's price tick after the AC-1 price rounding (an open or close more than `1e-8` off a tick is a bad row), volume at or above zero and on its size step after the AC-1 rounding (a volume more than `1e-8` off a step is a bad row), `high` ≥ max(open, close), `low` ≤ min(open, close), the timestamp on a whole minute inside the month, and no two different candles for one minute. A quiet candle must have open = high = low = close = the close of the latest earlier candle. For a month's first rows that is the previous month's last stored candle. When the previous month has no manifest (not fetched yet, or before `2022-03`), the first quiet rows are only checked for open = high = low = close, and the command logs WARNING `lab_candles_quiet_unchecked` with the month. A `--refetch` of a month also reruns this check on the next month's quiet rows up to its first trade minute, if that month is stored, and treats a failure there as a bad row of the next month (exit 1, the refetched month still commits). Every funding row is checked: stamp on a whole hour, and on a whole 4 h UTC boundary (00, 04, 08, 12, 16, 20) when before `FUNDING_HOURLY_FROM`; rate a finite decimal; no two different rates for one charge time, between the fetched or imported rows and the stored ones (AC-2 merge rule). Any failure stops the command with exit 1 and logs ERROR `lab_candles_bad_rows` listing every bad row of that month (minute, field, value). No file and no manifest is written for that month, and months already committed stay.
- **AC-4** (gaps): a run of five or more consecutive minutes with no candle is written as a row (`start_ns`, `end_ns`, `minutes`) in `gaps/<YYYY-MM>.parquet`. Missing minutes are never filled with made up candles. A quiet minute is a stored row, so it is neither missing nor part of a gap. The manifest holds `month`, `bar_rows`, `quiet_minutes`, `volume_rounded_rows`, `price_rounded_rows` (AC-1; a required field: a manifest without it, written before the field existed, counts as no manifest everywhere, so `fetch-candles` fetches that month again without `--refetch`, the `--refetch` next month quiet check treats it as not stored, and `lab backtest` lists it as a missing month (AC-6, exit 2) with the hint to run `fetch-candles` for it; the reader raises a typed `CandleInputError` for it, never a bare `TypeError`), `zero_volume_trade_rows`, `funding_rows`, `funding_sources` (the sorted distinct `source` values of the month's stored funding rows), `first_ts_ns`, `last_ts_ns`, `missing_minutes`, `gap_count`, `missing_funding_charges` (the `expected_charge_times` from `first_ts_ns` to `last_ts_ns` with no stored row; 0 for a month with no candles), `complete`, and `fetched_at` (UTC ISO 8601, the last candle fetch; an import never changes it).
- **AC-5** (network): requests are public GETs with no credentials, made with the Python standard library (`urllib.request`), a 30 s timeout, and a pause of `LAB_KRAKEN_PAUSE_MS` (default 250) between requests. A timeout, connection error, HTTP 429, or 5xx is retried up to 5 times, waiting 1, 2, 4, 8, then 16 s, and logs `lab_fetch_retry` each time. When retries run out, or on any other 4xx or a body that is not the expected JSON, the command logs ERROR `lab_fetch_failed` (URL without query, status, reason) and exits 1. Committed months stay and the failed month has no manifest. Before each month, the catalog cap check of spec 0008 AC-2 runs with the candles tree counted (expected month size 0.1 GB), and exits 3 when it would pass `LAB_CATALOG_CAP_GB`.
- **AC-6** (backtest input): `lab backtest <runfile> [--from D] [--to D] [--seed N] [--sweep-slippage] [--lookahead-check]` validates the run file (the model under *Run file*) and every override at startup. A bad file or value exits 64 with every problem listed, before anything is read or written. The range is UTC days, both inclusive, and `from` must be on or after `CANDLE_RANGE_FLOOR` (2022-03-24, a named constant; exit 64 before it). It then checks that every month the range touches has a manifest, that the range starts on or after the day of the first stored minute and ends no later than the last fetched minute of the last month, and the funding coverage: every expected charge time strictly inside the range (AC-4's rule: hourly from `FUNDING_HOURLY_FROM`, every 4 h before it) must have a row, or a carried rate, which is the latest row before it when that row's `ts_init` is at most `FUNDING_CARRY_MAX` (3 h, a named constant) earlier. A funding row must also exist with `ts_init` in `[range start − FUNDING_CARRY_MAX, range start]` (the same inclusive limit as the carry rule; it warms the strategy's market context through `warm_market_context` before the first bar, so the first entry is not refused as `funding_stale`). Otherwise it lists every missing month, the first stored minute or last fetched minute, or the first ten charge times with no rate inside the carry limit, and exits 2, writing no row, report, or bundle. When any of those charge times lies before the first stored row with `source` `api`, the message adds a hint to run `lab import-funding` with Kraken's funding export.
- **AC-7** (variants and grid): with no flag the run has one variant, `base`. `[strategy.grid]` maps strategy config fields (not also set in `[strategy.config]`, not run fields) to non empty lists. Their cross product, in sorted key order with each list in file order, gives variants `grid-1`, `grid-2`, … (at most 16, else exit 64), replacing `base`. `--sweep-slippage` adds `slippage=0` (0 bps) and `slippage=2x` (twice `slippage_bps`) next to `base`. It is refused with a grid (exit 64) and adds nothing when `slippage_bps` is 0. Each variant is its own `strategy_runs` row with its own `strategy_versions` row (the config hash includes the grid values), and `run_config` carries `batch_id`, `variant`, and `grid_point` (the grid values, or `{}`). The expansion is pure, in `trading_core/lab/variants.py` as a new `CandleVariantSpec` (name, `slippage_bps`, `grid_point`), since spec 0008's `VariantSpec` is built around latency and queue. Variants start in plan order and their bundles and reports are written in that order, as spec 0008 AC-6. The worker pool runs one variant per unit, at most `LAB_WORKERS` at a time.
- **AC-8** (engine): each variant runs in one fresh `BacktestEngine` over the whole range (from the first minute of `from` to the last minute of `to`), with no split at midnights or gaps. The venue is `KRAKEN` with `book_type=L1_MBP`, `oms_type=NETTING`, `account_type=MARGIN`, `MakerTakerFeeModel` on a copy of the pinned instrument with the run's `maker_fee` and `taker_fee`, no latency model, `queue_position=False`, `liquidity_consumption=False`, `trade_execution=False`, `bar_execution=False`, `use_reduce_only=True`, and the run's `starting_balance_usd` and `leverage`. `trader_id` is `BACKTESTER-LAB`.
- **AC-9** (candle stream): the stream turns each stored 1 minute candle (open time `m`) into four path points at `m + 1 ms` (open), `m + 20 s` (first extreme), `m + 40 s` (second extreme), `m + 59.999 s` (close). The extremes' order follows the strategy's net position as of the last processed path point when the minute is generated: long, low first; short, high first; flat, whichever extreme is nearer the open first (the high on a tie). A small `PositionSideFlag` actor sets that side from the engine's position events into a plain object the stream reads, and the stream builds one minute per chunk. Stated exception: in the minute an entry fills, the order was chosen while flat, so stop first is not guaranteed inside that one minute. Gate G-3 proves the flag lags by at most one path point. At each point it first emits a `TradeTick` at the point's `ts_init` and price, `NO_AGGRESSOR`, `trade_id` = `<ts_init>-<point index>`, with size = max(size step, volume ÷ 4 floored to the size step) at the first three points and max(size step, the remainder) at the close (so the four sizes may add up to more than the candle's volume, which only affects bar volume). Then it emits a `QuoteTick` at the same `ts_init` with bid = price × (1 − s) rounded down to the tick and ask = price × (1 + s) rounded up to the tick, where s = the variant's `slippage_bps` ÷ 10 000, and both sizes `QUOTE_SIZE` (a named constant, 1000 BTC at the size precision, far above any order `check_entry` can approve; the pinned instrument has no maximum quantity). The trade comes before the quote so that an L1 book update from the trade can never erase the slippage spread (gate G-3 asserts a market buy fills at the ask). A quiet minute (AC-1) emits only one `QuoteTick` at `m + 59.999 s` around its price (the same spread and sizes) and no trade, so no volume is invented, quotes stay as fresh as the real market's, and an hour made only of quiet minutes builds a zero volume bar (AC-11). The side flag plays no part in a quiet minute (one point, no extremes). The stream emits nothing before the range's first trade minute, so no bar is ever built before a real traded price exists (quiet minutes at the range start are skipped, and the report's first decision time shows it). A minute with no candle emits nothing. Funding rows are merged in by `ts_init` (before the trade at the same nanosecond). After the range's last minute, the stream emits one final quote repeating the last close at the last point + 2 ms (AC-14). The stream holds at most one month of candles in memory and is fed through `add_data_iterator`.
- **AC-10** (fills): for a strategy that decides on a bar close, a market buy fills at the ask of the path point current when the order reaches the venue (the minute's close point for an order sent on the close), and a sell at the bid. So every market fill pays `slippage_bps` against the candle price, plus the taker fee. A stop market order triggers on the first path point whose quote crosses its trigger, and fills at that quote. A limit order fills only when the opposite quote crosses its price (price trades through by the slippage, never a touch), and pays the maker fee. A position whose stop and target both lie inside one candle's range closes at the stop (the path order of AC-9, with its entry minute exception). Gate G-3 confirms in the engine that a triggered stop fills at the quote, not at its trigger price, and that a resting limit fills only on trade through. If the engine behaves otherwise, this AC comes back to `/architect` before Milestone 2.
- **AC-11** (same code): `lab backtest` runs the very `KfsStrategy` subclass the paper loop would run, built from the run file's `[strategy.config]` through the lab registry (spec 0008 AC-7 rules: the trader's own constructor arguments, a loaded and unpaused `PauseGate`, the real `EntryBlocks`, the `UnitRows` record callable, `check_entry` unchanged with the run's `RiskLimits`, the `LabRecorder` actor, no subclass or patch). Each candle strategy's bars come from its config field `bar_spec` (for example `1-HOUR-LAST`; only a time step of `MINUTE`, `HOUR`, or `DAY` with price type `LAST` is accepted, anything else exits 64), subscribed as `<instrument_id>-<bar_spec>-INTERNAL`, so the strategy aggregates bars from trade ticks the same way in the candle lab, in the order book replay, and live. An interval with no trades still closes a bar (open = high = low = close = the last price, volume 0), because the data engine keeps NautilusTrader's default `time_bars_build_with_no_updates = true`, the same as the paper trader. Strategies see that bar, and the report counts them as `zero_volume_bars` (AC-16). The 2026-10-17 engine gate found that an hour with no candles at all builds this bar too. A strategy with `use_market_context` receives the funding rows through its usual handler (mark, index, open interest, and liquidations are absent and read as missing). Registry entries carry a strategy kind. `buy_and_hold` and `macd_baseline` are `hour_level`, and `lab backtest` refuses (exit 64) a strategy whose kind is not `hour_level`.
- **AC-12** (buy and hold): `buy_and_hold` (pure rule in `trading_core/strategy/buy_and_hold.py`, node class `trading_core/node/buy_and_hold.py`) buys once at the first `bar_spec` bar close of the run, with `fixed_quantity` = `notional_usd` ÷ the last mid, floored to the size step, through `check_entry`, and holds with no stop until the range end close. Config: `bar_spec` (default `1-HOUR-LAST`), `notional_usd` (required), `max_entry_attempts` (default 24), `use_market_context` true. When `check_entry` refuses, it tries again at the next bar close and records each refusal as a decision, at most `max_entry_attempts` times. After that it logs `entry_abandoned` once and stays flat for the rest of the run.
- **AC-13** (MACD baseline): `macd_baseline` (pure rules and indicators in `trading_core/strategy/macd_baseline.py` and `trading_core/strategy/indicators.py`, node class `trading_core/node/macd_baseline.py`) runs on each `bar_spec` bar close while flat. It enters long when the MACD histogram (fast EMA − slow EMA, minus its signal EMA, all on closes) crosses from ≤ 0 to > 0 and the close is above the trend EMA, and short in the mirror case. Long exits at market when the histogram is below its previous value, short when it is above (the histogram weakens). Entries use `fixed_quantity` = `notional_usd` ÷ mid through `check_entry`, judged with a stop `stop_pct` percent from the mid. When the entry fills, the real protective stop market order is placed `stop_pct` percent from the fill price (so the stop checked and the stop placed can differ by the fill's slippage). Config: `bar_spec` (`1-HOUR-LAST`), `fast` 12, `slow` 26, `signal` 9, `trend_ema` 50, `stop_pct` `"3"`, `notional_usd` (required), `use_market_context` true. Each EMA uses `Decimal` with α = 2 ÷ (n + 1), seeded with the simple mean of its first n inputs. No decision is made until the histogram and trend EMA both have a value (warmup happens inside the range, 50 bars for the trend EMA with the defaults, and the report shows the first decision's time).

  Shared by both strategies: every `EntryRequest` field is sourced as `PlaceholderTimed` sources it (equity from the account balance plus `guard.unrealized`, `open_notional` from `guard.open_notional`, `instrument_maintenance_rate` from `guard.margin_maint`, `size_step` from the instrument, `paused` and `halted` from the `PauseGate`, `blocks` from `EntryBlocks`, `funding_rate` from `market_context()`, `funding_periods` = `funding_periods(now, None)` = 1). Both use the stale quote block, which moves out of `PlaceholderTimed` into a shared mixin `StaleQuoteBlock` in `trading_core/node/stale.py`, configured by two strategy config fields, `stale_quote_s` (paper default 10) and `stale_check_s` (how often the 1 s timer `STALE_CHECK_NS` runs today; paper default 1). `PlaceholderTimed` uses the mixin with the same defaults and must behave exactly as before (its existing stale feed tests pass unchanged, plus a regression test). Candle runs take `stale_quote_s` from the run file (default 300, must be above 60) and `stale_check_s` = 60 (`CANDLE_STALE_CHECK_S`, a named constant), because path points are about 20 s apart, a quiet minute has one quote, and a 1 s timer would add about 140 million timer events to a full history run. So entries are blocked only inside a real gap of about 5 missing minutes (the AC-4 gap rule, give or take the 60 s check), never between the points of a normal minute. Both read funding through `market_context()` with the candle run file's `[context].max_age_funding_s` (default 18 000 s, one 4 h funding period plus 1 h), so a rate stays fresh across a 4 h period and a carried hour (AC-6), and a strategy is refused as `funding_stale` only inside a hole AC-6 would have refused. Both handle a position closed from any source (their own exit, the range end closer, or the liquidation guard): they cancel their resting stop and reset their entry state.
- **AC-14** (range end, liquidation, ruin): the range end closer (the spec 0008 AC-8 closer, with the reason passed in) fires at the last path point + 1 ms. It cancels the strategy's resting orders and closes any open position with a market order, after recording a `decisions` row with intent `exit` and reason `range_end`. The order fills against the final quote of AC-9 (the last close, at + 2 ms), so the close matches the buy and hold benchmark's last price. A `LiquidationGuard` actor watches every quote while a position is open. Its liquidation price comes from `trading_core.risk.liquidation.liquidation_price` with: side mapped to `recording.enums.OrderSide`; the position's quantity and average entry price; `equity` = the `figures.equity` of the approved entry decision's `check_entry` verdict, read from the strategy's recorded decision; other notional 0; `fee` = `taker_fee × quantity × entry price` in USD; and `rate` = the run's `maintenance_margin_rate` through `maintenance_rate` with the instrument's rate. Funding is not part of the engine's equity (the lab books it after the run), so it does not move the liquidation price. When a long's bid falls to or below it, or a short's ask rises to or above it, the guard records a decision with intent `exit` and reason `liquidated`, then closes the position at market on that quote through the strategy's own `order_factory` and `submit_order` (as `SegmentEndCloser` does). The report counts `liquidations`, and the strategy may enter again later (tested: a liquidation followed by a new entry). If account equity falls to zero or below at any point, the runner flattens, ends the variant early, and the report says `ruined: true` with the time. This is a result, not a failure.
- **AC-15** (funding): at every charge time from `expected_charge_times` strictly inside the range (AC-2), an open position pays `−signed_qty × mark_px × rate` through the existing `LabRecorder` samples and `trading_core.lab.funding.apply_funding`, with `mark_px` the mid of the last quote at or before the charge time and `rate` picked by `rate_at` from the candles tree's funding rows (so a missing row is charged at the carried rate AC-6 allowed). The candle path passes `expected_charge_times` to `LabRecorder` (its `funding_boundaries`) and to the funding pass in place of the hourly `funding_boundaries` that `lab replay` keeps using. The funding pass asserts that every carried rate is at most `FUNDING_CARRY_MAX` old (a failure fails the variant, AC-18), and counts `carried_funding_charges` = expected charge times in the range with no exact row, whether or not a position was open. Each stored rate is charged once at its charge time; a G-4 per hour finding is handled at import (AC-2a), never here. Each payment is a `funding_payments` row (source `computed`) and counts in `positions.funding_total`, `run_results.funding_total`, and the net P&L, exactly as spec 0008 AC-9. `run_config.funding_source` is `"kraken_export+api"`.
- **AC-16** (results): before its first variant, `lab backtest` upserts the pinned instrument row and, per variant, the strategy version (kind `hour_level`). A completed variant calls `write_run_bundle` once as `lab_app` with mode `backtest`, env `demo`, status `completed`, its decisions, risk events, orders, order events, fills, positions, funding payments, and one `run_results` row (sample `full`, from `trading_core.lab.metrics.run_metrics`), with no `run_fill_stats`. `run_config` holds the spec 0008 fields that apply (`batch_id`, `variant`, `run_file`, `run_file_sha256`, `overrides`, `seed`, `instrument_id`, fees, `starting_balance_usd`, `leverage`, `risk_limits`, `nautilus_version`, `git_commit`) plus `source: "candles"`, `slippage_bps`, `grid_point`, `bar_type` (the stored bar type), `funding_source`, `candle_gaps` (each gap row overlapping the range), and `range` (`[start_ns, end_ns]`). `run_results.max_drawdown` keeps spec 0008's meaning (realized equity only) and is labelled "realized" in the report. The report JSON and Markdown add, from pure functions in `trading_core.lab.candle_metrics`: a marked to market equity curve (sampled at every hour close: realized equity plus the open position's P&L at that close's mid, plus funding booked so far) with its max drawdown and the longest drawdown duration (hours from a peak until equity is back above it, or until the range end); long and short split (closed trades and net P&L each); exposure time (share of the range with a position open); profit factor (gross profit ÷ gross loss, NULL when there is no loss); expectancy per closed trade (net P&L ÷ trade count); `liquidations`, `ruined`, and the first decision time; and a data quality block: the span covered; gap rows overlapping the range; per month touched (labelled so, not prorated to the range) missing minutes, quiet minutes, rounded volume rows, zero volume trade rows, and missing funding charges, from the manifests; `carried_funding_charges` from the funding pass (AC-15); and `zero_volume_bars`, the strategy's `bar_spec` bars with volume 0 closed inside the range, from quiet hours or hours with no candles alike. `LabRecorder` counts them: it takes the strategy's internal bar type in its constructor, subscribes to it (the same bar type the strategy subscribes to, so no second aggregator; gate G-3b checks this), and adds the count to `CandleUnitResult`. These live in the report only, not in `run_results`. It writes `lab-reports/<batch_id>/<variant>.md`, `.json`, and `.bundle.json`, then `index.md` listing every variant side by side with net P&L, return after costs, realized and marked max drawdown, fees, funding, trade count, exposure time, liquidations, first decision time, and the buy and hold benchmark (`(last close − first close) ÷ first close` of the range's candles, before costs, shown for comparison only).
- **AC-17** (determinism): two runs with the same run file, overrides, data, and seed give identical report JSON (except `run_id`, `batch_id`, `started_at`, `ended_at`) and identical rows apart from ids and those timestamps, with `LAB_WORKERS` 1 or 2. This is the spec 0008 AC-12 rule applied to the candle path: `CandleUnitPlan` and `CandleUnitResult` (new, since spec 0008's `UnitPlan` and `UnitResult` are shaped around segments and hours) and the candle plan, bundle, and `run_config` builders in `apps/lab/src/lab/candle_backtest.py`, merging rows by (`ts`, sequence within the unit).
- **AC-18** (failure and interruption): a variant fails, is isolated, and is reported with the same rules as spec 0008 AC-13, through the candle path of AC-17 and the existing `UnitPool` (no database rows, `status: failed`, ERROR `lab_variant_failed`, the others still run, exit 1 if any failed). SIGINT or SIGTERM ends the command with exit 130 after writing `interrupted` reports and `index.md`.
- **AC-19** (look ahead): a strategy never receives a bar before the last path point of that bar's final minute has been fed. With `--lookahead-check`, the batch's first variant (`base` or `grid-1`; look ahead is a property of the code, not of a parameter value), after its full run, is run again three times on the same data cut at three times drawn from `random.Random(seed)` uniformly over the range's whole hours (excluding the first and last 10 %). Every decision of the full run with `decided_ns` strictly before a cut (`decided_ns < cut`), other than `range_end`, must equal one of the cut run (same intent, side, reason, `decided_ns`, risk verdict) and the cut run must have no extra one. A mismatch fails that variant (AC-18 rules), logs ERROR `lab_lookahead_mismatch` with the cut and the first differing decision, and the report shows it. A pytest test runs the same prefix check on every registered candle strategy over a seeded synthetic month of candles cut at five points. The check catches any peek at data the engine has not yet fed (through the stream, the strategy's buffers, or `SwingTracker`); it cannot catch a strategy that reads data from outside the engine, which the registry rule (strategies get data only through the engine) forbids.
- **AC-20** (swing helper): `trading_core/strategy/swings.py` provides pure `SwingTracker(left, right)` with `update(index, high, low) -> list[Swing]`. A swing high at bar `i` is a bar whose high is strictly above the highs of the `left` bars before it and the `right` bars after it (a swing low mirrors it with lows). It is returned only from the `update` call for bar `i + right`, as `Swing(kind, bar_index=i, price, confirmed_index=i + right)`, never earlier. Unit tests prove that no swing is ever returned before its confirming bar, and that feeding a prefix of the bars returns exactly the swings of the full feed whose `confirmed_index` lies inside the prefix.
- **AC-21** (parity): `lab candle-parity <runfile> [--from D] [--to D]` plans the `own` order book segments of the range (spec 0008 AC-4 planner). For each segment, it runs the strategy in memory twice over the same start and end: once through the spec 0008 order book unit (base variant, the run's fees, `queue_position` true, latency from the run file's optional `[parity]` table, `latency_ms` default 25) and once through the candle unit of this spec. `candle-parity` builds the order book side's unit plans from the own tree itself (it does not go through `lab replay`'s run file or its strategy kind rule, which stay unchanged). `[parity]` is allowed in a candle run file and read only by `candle-parity`. It matches `enter` decisions, approved or refused: same side and `decided_ns` within 60 s. It prints matches ÷ max(order book decisions, candle decisions) and every unmatched decision, writes nothing to Postgres, exits 1 under 0.95, and exits 2 when own days or candle months are missing or no decision is left to compare. The committed run file `apps/lab/runs/macd_parity.toml` runs `macd_baseline` with `bar_spec = "1-MINUTE-LAST"` (enough decisions over a few recorded days) and `use_market_context = false`, so both sides see the same inputs (the order book side would otherwise also get mark, index, open interest, and liquidations). Only `enter` decisions are compared. One missed cross can shift the following entries (a `position_open` refusal on one side only), which the 0.95 threshold has to absorb, and verify.md records the unmatched list.
- **AC-22** (guards): the lab still exits 78 with `TRADING_ENV=live` and on an image built without `GIT_COMMIT`. `fetch-candles`, `import-funding`, and `candle-parity` never connect to Postgres (run with `--no-deps`), and `backtest` connects only as `lab_app`, whose existing grants cover every table it writes (no migration). No Kraken key is mounted or read, and every Kraken request is an unauthenticated public GET to `LAB_KRAKEN_FUTURES_URL`.
- **AC-23** (speed and memory): one variant of `buy_and_hold` over the full history (2022-03-24 to the last complete month) finishes in under 60 minutes on the laptop (the gate measured about 6,000 events a second, about 53 minutes for 55 months; one minute chunks are what keep the stop first order, so they are not traded for speed), with the events per second of each month recorded, and its `ru_maxrss` stays under 1.5 GB inside the `lab` container's `mem_limit: 3g`. A full fetch of every month from 2022-03 finishes in under 30 minutes. A 16 point grid over the full history with `LAB_WORKERS=2`, and the same run with `--lookahead-check`, are timed too (measured only, no budget). All of these go into verify.md.

## Decision

**Chosen option**: Option 2: synthetic quote and trade path from 1 minute candles into the existing lab's `BacktestEngine`, with our own candle catalog fetched from Kraken's public history.

Store Kraken's 1 minute trade candles and hourly funding rates as monthly Parquet, turn each minute into four position aware price points with the slippage built into the quoted spread, and run the paper loop's strategy classes on them in a new `lab backtest` command that reuses the lab's pool, registry, recorder, funding, metrics, bundle, and reports.

**Implementation skills**: `nautilus-trader` (`algoderiv/agent-skills`, `.claude/skills/nautilus-trader/`) · `uv` (`terminalskills/skills`, `.claude/skills/uv/`) · `docker-compose-skill` (`scientiacapital/skills`, `.claude/skills/docker-compose-skill/`) · `sqlalchemy` (`terminalskills/skills`, `.claude/skills/sqlalchemy/`)

## Feature design

### Pipeline

```
Kraken charts API (1m trade candles) ─┐
Kraken historical funding API ────────┴─ lab fetch-candles ──► catalog-lab/candles/ (month files, gaps, manifests)
                                                                     ▼
run file (TOML) ──► lab backtest ──► range checks ──► variants (base | grid-n | slippage sweep)
                                                                     ▼
              worker pool (LAB_WORKERS, one spawned process per variant):
                 candle stream (4 path points per minute: trade, then quote with slippage spread,
                 funding rows) ──► BacktestEngine (L1, fees, same KfsStrategy + check_entry,
                 LabRecorder, PositionSideFlag, LiquidationGuard, range end closer) ──► CandleUnitResult
                                                                     ▼
              parent: funding, metrics, [look ahead cuts] ──► bundle ──► laptop Postgres (lab_app)
                                                                     └► lab-reports/<batch>/<variant>.md|.json|.bundle.json, index.md
own catalog + candles ──► lab candle-parity (in memory, order book unit vs candle unit)
```

### Data model sketch

**New Parquet tree `catalog-lab/candles/`** (laptop only, counted in `LAB_CATALOG_CAP_GB`):

| File | Rows | Fields | Rules |
|---|---|---|---|
| `data/bar/PF_XBTUSD.KRAKEN-1-MINUTE-LAST-EXTERNAL/<YYYY-MM>.parquet` | Nautilus `Bar` | open, high, low, close (Price, required), volume (Quantity, required, rounded per AC-1), ts_event = ts_init = minute close (ns, required), quiet (bool, required) | sorted by ts_init; one row per minute; AC-3 checks; never filled in |
| `data/funding_rate_update/PF_XBTUSD.KRAKEN/<YYYY-MM>.parquet` | Nautilus `FundingRateUpdate` | rate (Decimal), next_funding_ns = ts_init + period, ts_event = ts_init = charge time (ns), source (`export` or `api`) | one row per charge time in the month (by the charge time's month); merged from the export and the API, rows never deleted (AC-2); same table name as spec 0009 |
| `gaps/<YYYY-MM>.parquet` | gap rows | start_ns, end_ns (the missing span), minutes (int) | runs of 5+ missing minutes (AC-4) |
| `manifests/<YYYY-MM>.json` | one per month | AC-4 fields | written last; its presence commits the month |

Each month's manifest relates 1:N to its bar, funding, and gap rows. File writes go through `trading_core.catalog`'s `atomic_write`/`durable_replace`. The Parquet write and read helpers live in `trading_core/catalog/candles.py` (the only place besides `node/` allowed to touch Nautilus and pyarrow).

**Postgres: no migration.** Reused unchanged from spec 0002: `instruments`, `strategies` (kind `hour_level`), `strategy_versions` (one per variant config), `strategy_runs` (mode `backtest`, env `demo`, `trader_id` `BACKTESTER-LAB`), `run_results` (sample `full`), `decisions`, `orders`, `order_events`, `fills`, `positions`, `funding_payments` (source `computed`), `risk_events`. `run_config` contents are in AC-16. Code change: the `Store` protocol's `prepare` (and `PostgresStore.prepare` in `apps/lab/src/lab/replay.py`, plus the test fakes) gains a `kind` argument, taken from the registry entry instead of the hard coded `SCALPING`; `lab replay` passes its strategies' kind, which is still `scalping` for every strategy it runs today.

seed = 7

### Key invariants

- No money value is a float: candle prices, rates, slippage, and P&L are `Decimal`, `Price`, `Quantity`, or `Money` from the API string to the report.
- No candle is ever invented. A missing minute emits no tick. Apart from a noise snap of at most `1e-8`, no stored price lies beyond the high and low Kraken reported (AC-1 rounds off tick extremes toward the body).
- A bar reaches a strategy only after its last minute's close point (AC-19).
- Every market fill is at least `slippage_bps` worse than the candle price. A stop never fills better than its triggering quote.
- A complete month on disk is never changed without `--refetch`. A month without a manifest is never read.
- Strategy classes are never subclassed or patched for the lab.

### Security model

Laptop only, demo only. No secret beyond the existing `postgres_lab_password`. Kraken requests are public, unauthenticated GETs, and no key exists on the laptop (spec 0003). `lab_app` keeps SELECT and INSERT on the bundle tables only. No regulated data.

### Configuration required

- `LAB_KRAKEN_FUTURES_URL`: base URL of the public Kraken Futures API (default `https://futures.kraken.com`), validated as an https URL in `LabSettings`.
- `LAB_KRAKEN_PAUSE_MS`: pause between requests (default 250, 0 to 5 000).
- The candles tree lives under the existing `LAB_CATALOG_DIR` mount (`/catalog-lab/candles`), so the Compose service needs no new volume.

### Critical test scenarios

- Funding merge: import a fixture export (4 h rows before the switch, hourly after, one 3 h hole), then fetch overlapping API rows: nothing is duplicated or deleted, a differing rate exits 1, a second import adds nothing, charges land at stamp + period, the hole is carried and counted, verifies **AC-2**, **AC-2a**, **AC-6**, **AC-15**
- Quiet and noisy candles: a fixture month with float noise volumes and volume 0 flat minutes: volumes round and are counted, a quiet minute emits one quote and no trade, a quiet row that is not flat and a volume `1e-6` off the step are both bad rows, verifies **AC-1**, **AC-3**, **AC-9**
- Off tick prices: a high of `25920.8322615` is stored as 25920, a low of `28319.01027283563` as 28320, a high of `39522.99999999999` as 39523 and a low of `42938.99999999999` as 42939; an open of `100.00000001` snaps to 100 and one of `100.0000001` is a bad row; a candle with two fields off counts once in `price_rounded_rows`; a raw high of `99.99999999999` with close 100 is not a bad row; two page edge copies that differ only in noise are kept once; a volume 0 candle that rounds flat is quiet; a manifest without `price_rounded_rows` is fetched again and refused by `lab backtest` (exit 2), verifies **AC-1**, **AC-3**, **AC-4**, **AC-6**
- Stale block: with `stale_quote_s` 300 an hour close entry is approved, and after a 6 minute gap it is refused `blocked:stale_feed`, verifies **AC-13**
- Happy path: fetch two months, backtest `buy_and_hold` and `macd_baseline` over them, rows and reports written with fees, slippage, and funding, verifies **AC-1**, **AC-2**, **AC-12**, **AC-13**, **AC-15**, **AC-16**
- Fill rules on a hand built candle fixture: market at close ± slippage, stop first when both inside one candle, limit only on trade through, verifies **AC-9**, **AC-10**
- Look ahead: a test strategy that reads the next bar fails both the pytest prefix check and `--lookahead-check`, verifies **AC-19**
- Failure: a bad candle row stops the month with every bad row listed, a 429 storm retries then exits 1 keeping earlier months, verifies **AC-3**, **AC-5**
- Liquidation: a 2x long through a fixture crash closes at the guard with reason `liquidated`, verifies **AC-14**
- Permission: `TRADING_ENV=live` exits 78; `lab_app` cannot write outside the bundle tables, verifies **AC-22**

## Consequences

**Positive**:
- Four and a half years of the real contract, with Kraken's own funding since launch, for free, in under 1 GB.
- No volume is ever made up: a minute with no trades is a quote, not a trade.
- The paper loop's strategy classes run unchanged, and parity against the order book replay proves it numerically.
- Slippage, stop first ordering, and forced liquidation lean pessimistic, so a passing strategy is unlikely to be a flattering artifact.
- No migration: feature 15 reads candle results from the same tables as replay results.

**Negative / tradeoffs**:
- Four points per minute hide the true path. A stop fills at the minute's extreme, not at its trigger, which overstates stop losses in fast minutes.
- No order book means no queue or partial fills. Maker limit fills are approximate (trade through only), so maker heavy hour strategies should be checked in the replay lab too.
- Mark, index, open interest, and liquidations have no history before our recording, so strategies that need them cannot be backtested on years of data.
- Warmup inside the range spends the first bars of every range (50 bars, the trend EMA, for the MACD baseline: about two days on 1h bars).
- In the minute an entry fills, the path order was chosen while flat, so stop first is not guaranteed inside that single minute.
- The marked drawdown, exposure, and the other report only figures are not in `run_results`, so feature 15 reads them from the report or the bundle until it decides otherwise.
- BTC only. ETH needs its own pinned instrument and a later fetch.
- Funding before the endpoint's rolling year depends on a manual import of Kraken's export, which Kraken refreshes irregularly (the 2026-10-17 copy ends 2026-02-01). Funding rows are kept forever once stored, but a hole opens if neither `fetch-candles` runs within a year of a funding hour nor a newer export is imported.
- The 4 h funding era (before 2022-09-29 12:00) depends on gate G-4's reading of the rate. If it can't be settled, ranges start at 2022-09-30 and the 2022 crash months are lost.
- A full history run takes up to an hour.
- Quiet minutes keep quotes at the last trade price, which may lag the real quotes in a thin market (mostly March and April 2022).
- Depends on a Kraken endpoint that may change shape or limits. The gate and the row checks catch it, but a change means a code fix.

**Neutral**:
- `lab replay`'s strategy kind stops being hard coded (a small change to spec 0008 code).
- New files in core: `catalog/candles.py`, `lab/candles.py`, `lab/candle_path.py`, `lab/candle_metrics.py`, `strategy/indicators.py`, `strategy/swings.py`, `strategy/buy_and_hold.py`, `strategy/macd_baseline.py`, `node/buy_and_hold.py`, `node/macd_baseline.py`.
- `apps/lab/AGENTS.md` needs the new commands and the candles tree (for `/sync`).
